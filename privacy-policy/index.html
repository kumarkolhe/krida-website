Mirror Twins: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Mirror Twins ("the game"), made by KRID A GAMES ("we", "us"), collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (scores, XP, unlocked levels, gems, skins, daily gift streak), settings (sound, music, haptics, tutorial tips, ad consent) and which purchases you own are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

2. Online leaderboards

To show global leaderboards, the game signs you in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). This creates an anonymous player ID. We store:

  • your anonymous player ID,
  • your leaderboard name (a randomly generated name, or a nickname you choose), and
  • your best score on each level.

Your leaderboard name and scores are visible to other players. We use this information only to run the leaderboards. It is kept until you ask us to delete it (see section 7).

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, including ironSource and Unity Ads) and the ad networks it works with. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these partners collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location (derived from your IP address),
  • how you interact with ads, and
  • technical and diagnostic information about your device and the app.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads may be personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Personalised ads. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings. Watching an ad for a reward is always optional.

See the Unity Privacy Policy for how Unity and its partners handle this data.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what you bought so we can unlock it. See the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing your score

When you tap Share, the game creates an image of your score and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app to share it with.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the online features, contact us and we will delete their data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your online data: in the game, open Settings → Delete online data. This deletes your anonymous player account and removes your name from the leaderboards; any scores left on a leaderboard are then shown as "Removed player" and are no longer linked to you. To have those scores removed completely, email us (include the leaderboard name you used, if you remember it) and we will delete them within 30 days.
  • Ads: change personalised-ads consent in Settings (EEA/UK/Switzerland), or use your device's advertising ID settings.
  • Access, correction, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Information sent by the game and its service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
